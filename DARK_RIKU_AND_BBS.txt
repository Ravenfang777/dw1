Yes, BBS has useful source data. OpenKH documents:
- PAM: animation entries, frame count/rate/looping and per-bone channels.
- PMO: model skeletons and weighted geometry.
- AAC / ABC: body / weapon collision and effects.
- FEP particles and SEB/SEI/SCD audio-related files are additional effect sources.

This needs a new BBS importer and skeleton retargeter. The current KH2 loader
cannot read BBS data directly. Inspect the user's BBS release and package layout
before assuming PSP-format details apply unchanged to a remaster. Start with
animation/body/weapon tracks, then adapt root motion, attack windows/damage,
commands, particles and sound separately. Having these files does not establish
that BBS combo rules, AI or spell behavior can be copied unchanged.
No BBS importer, assets or new BBS moveset is included in v39.

Primary format sources:
https://openkh.dev/bbs/file/type/pam.html
https://openkh.dev/bbs/file/type/pmo.html
