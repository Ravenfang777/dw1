SETTINGS
kh1-overdrive.ini supports #RRGGBB colors. v77 reads palette3. Version2's exact
old Control #00008B and Will #800080 defaults upgrade automatically; other
colors and custom values remain. Older pre-version2 settings upgrade to the
complete current defaults. A menu selection save persists version3. No settings
reset is needed. Restart after manual color edits. Example:
palette_version = 3
overdrive = 6
control_color = #0055FF
will_color = #400060
talent_color = #FF10F0
might_color = #8B0000
vigor_color = #CC5500
resistance_color = #FFFF00
immunity_color = #228B22
prowess_color = #F5F5FF
spite_color = #202020
