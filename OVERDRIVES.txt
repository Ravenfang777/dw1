Customize -> Overdrives
Power - Red #FF0000
Will - Blue #0000FF
Resistance - Yellow #FFFF00
Brilliance - White #FFFFFF (formerly Prowess; same ID4)
Rage - Black #000000

All five share Valor plus Wisdom wind. Wisdom foot11/12 and shared Anti
darkness are removed. The normal Anti/Wisdom stance effects are separate.
One free selection, independent of Stance; select its active row to turn off.

Two row panels show Drain and5. This value is for display/testing only:
no AP/HP/MP/resource drain is implemented. No Overdrive AP Used footer.

Optional kh1-overdrive.ini settings beside the other mod settings:
overdrive = 1
power_color = #FF0000
will_color = #0000FF
resistance_color = #FFFF00
brilliance_color = #FFFFFF
rage_color = #000000

0 is off;1..5 follow the list above. Colors use six hexadecimal RGB digits.
Restart after manual edits. Menu choices preserve custom colors and unknown
keys. Old strength_color and prowess_color are aliases; current power_color
and brilliance_color take precedence. Existing selection/settings need no reset.
The ZIP does not overwrite existing settings. Missing keys use defaults and
are appended on the next selection save. No texture extraction is needed.

Check OVERDRIVE SHARED LAYER READY for Valor and Wisdom; Wisdom should list100.
Live particles, black blending and the exact removed/retained visuals need an
in-game check. See source/STYLES-V72.MD for sources, confidence and test scope.
