Customize -> Overdrives
Power - Red #FF0000 (renamed from Strength; same selection ID1)
Will - Blue #0000FF
Resistance - Yellow #FFFF00
Prowess - White #FFFFFF
Rage - Black #000000

All five share Valor + Wisdom + Anti body-effect layers, tinted to their color.
Master/Final archives are not needed. Check OVERDRIVE SHARED LAYER READY for
all three donors in kh1-dual-wield.log. Missing layers are logged individually.

Cost0 AP, no native ability slot, one selected at a time. Choose the active
row again to turn it off. Stance and numbered Passives remain independent.
Visuals only; stat boosts will be assigned later.

OPTIONAL CUSTOM COLORS
kh1-overdrive.ini beside other mod settings accepts these values:
overdrive = 1
power_color = #FF0000
will_color = #0000FF
resistance_color = #FFFF00
prowess_color = #FFFFFF
rage_color = #000000

overdrive0 is off;1..5 follow the list above. Use six hexadecimal RGB digits.
Restart the game after editing colors. Selecting another row preserves custom
colors and unknown keys. Missing color keys use the requested defaults and
are appended on the next menu selection save. Existing settings are not
overwritten by installing this ZIP. Old strength_color is accepted unless
power_color is also present. No asset extraction/image editing is needed.

Cloned grayscale/alpha-mask textures remove donor color bias; the source
stance/spell resources stay unchanged. Black uses alpha instead of invisible
black additive blending. Wrist/palm alignment and bounded aura geometry are
implemented; final hand tracking, layered brightness and Rage appearance
still need an in-game check. See source/STYLES-V71.MD for validation scope.
